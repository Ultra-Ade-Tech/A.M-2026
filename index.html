The power of
perseverance

Monsurat Omolara, adeleke biography.

My Mother needed a bachelors degree. She started by being a midwife in Nigeria, then proceeded to obtain her bachelors degree in nursing in the Philippines. She worked for 3 years as a RM before leaving Philippines and moved to the USA to study as a nurse after passing her NCLEX board exams. She needed to be eligible to enrol for the NP program, she needed to apply for a different University. She had different NP programs such as: pmhnp, pnp, cnm, agaenp, and fnp. She had so many choices but she decided to go with pmhnp. She chose that route because she enjoyed Psychiatry, it's her passion to help people with mental problems. The program was 2 and a half years full time. It includes lots of training, assignments, reading, exams, and practicum. She needed to complete many board exams to become certified. Candidates got to choose to unite with ANCC or ANPP. She chose ANCC and passed on her first try! She became a board certified PMHNP!
